Lore

Bitten by a radioactive spider, high school student Peter Parker gained the proportional strength and agility of an arachnid. After the tragic loss of his Uncle Ben, Peter learned that with great power comes great responsibility.

In Marvel Rivals, Spider-Man joins forces with heroes across time and dimensions to halt the destructive collision of universes orchestrated by Doom 2099 and Doctor Doom.

Spider-Man relies heavily on momentum and mobility, making him the most agile duelist in the game.

Strenghts and weaknesses

AGILE DIVE HERO

An incredibly agile and complex dive hero capable of assaulting and eliminating enemies from the air with his combos, and then swiftly leaves the scene.

BURST DAMAGE

His low damage output over time means, he has to be very accurate with his abilities to maximize off of his burst damage through combos.

SURVIVABILITY

His low survivability and HP means he has to use his movement abilities effectively to survive and escape. Mastery of his kit creates an oppressive character.

FIGHTING STYLE

Spider-Man is the most unique character in Marvel Rivals. Stringing together abilities to perform combos makes him play more like a fighting game than a hero shooter.
